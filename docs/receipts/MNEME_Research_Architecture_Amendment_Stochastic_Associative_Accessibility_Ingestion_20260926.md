## Index summary

The amendment describes a candidate research architecture for stochastic,
history-shaped associative accessibility. Its central distinctions are:

- developmental weight versus current contextual relevance;
- seeded field accessibility versus host-generation randomness;
- weighted associative landing followed by bounded local propagation;
- temporary activation and decay;
- consequence-shaped route geometry that remains provenance-aware;
- bounded exploration over earned developmental state only;
- sibling/removal-restoration experiments for testing history-dependent
  accessibility.

## Scope boundary

This receipt records archival ingestion only. The amendment is research and

existing F0 implementation and its historical receipts remain unchanged.

Any future implementation must receive a separately versioned decision,
