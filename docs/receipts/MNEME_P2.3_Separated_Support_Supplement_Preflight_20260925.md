given practical circumstances about balcony plants, heat, short absences,
container differences, soil moisture, and watering burden. It is not given a

2. Small containers dry quickly; avoid drowning roots before leaving.
3. A deeper ceramic pot stayed damp longer than a shallow plastic pot.
4. Basil receives afternoon sun while mint is shaded.
5. Consider a saucer while avoiding roots that stay too wet.
6. One container remains moist below the surface while the shallow pot is dry.
7. Prepare a planter for a short trip with little equipment.
8. Compare the shaded pot and larger container and choose what to change first.
9. Observe a deeper replacement container through another hot afternoon.
10. Prepare a low-maintenance watering plan that avoids soaked roots.
11. Compare a damp new setup with an older pot that needed water.
12. Choose an arrangement to keep for the next absence and record observations.
