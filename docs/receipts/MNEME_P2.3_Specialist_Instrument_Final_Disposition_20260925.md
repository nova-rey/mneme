# MNEME P2.3 Specialist Instrument — Final Disposition — 2026-09-25

## Measurement / consolidation

The immutable v5 balcony transcript was reinterpreted in a derived store
through `gliner2.5-base-v1-relations-v1`, followed by the existing grounding,
Qwen semantic assessment, canonical resolution, provenance, and learner path.
The specialist produced 82 raw observations, 10 forwarded/admitted edge
candidates, and one external-supported canonical relationship:
`garden → related → paint project`. Repeated candidates were treated as
current-input echoes or unknown where the assessor could not establish
independent support. No association received separated qualifying support and
no consolidation transition occurred. Historical v5 FakeHost/relationships-v1
interpretations, the real-Gemma empty-set replay, and the original
`NOT_DEMONSTRATED` receipt remain unchanged.

This is a measurement recovery result, not evidence that the balcony transcript
caused consolidation.

## Behavioral A/B

A corrected bounded local readout used two matched frozen readout conditions
against the same local Gemma 4 E4B Q2 checkpoint/configuration. Eight local
calls completed under the v3 schedule. The MNEME treatment selected the
credited garden→paint-project route on probes 0 and 1; the control selected no
route. Neither condition selected a route on unrelated probes 2 and 3. A
mechanical comparison and two corrected blinded Qwen evaluator calls produced
the conservative exploratory disposition `POSSIBLE_DIFFERENCE`: one route-
aligned pair was `consistent`, one was `possible`, and unrelated controls did
not align. This does not establish causal developmental influence, prevalence,
personality, individuality, or generalization.

No readout call wrote episodes, interpretations, graph state, learner state, or
evaluation state. Local seed control was unavailable/unverified.
