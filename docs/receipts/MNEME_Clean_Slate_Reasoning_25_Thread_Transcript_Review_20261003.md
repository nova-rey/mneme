At the same time, other ON answers are useful but diffuse, and the receipt does not preserve rejected proposition text to separate those cases mechanically. OFF’s higher density of concrete relational wording therefore explains part of the gap, while pipeline undercapture of qualified/synthesized ON content explains another part.
