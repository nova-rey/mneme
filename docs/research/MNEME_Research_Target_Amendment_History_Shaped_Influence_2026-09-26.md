## History-shaped influence: measure how experience changes the next response

**Current-work boundary:** No change to a running experiment, approved learner rule, historical result, release criterion, or execution budget.

> **The central question is not whether the instance can announce where it has been. It is whether having been there changes what occurs to it next.**

## 1. The research target to preserve

MNEME investigates whether accumulated external experience and the instance’s own observable behavior can produce persistent, history-shaped differences in what a frozen model notices, connects, prioritizes, and expresses when encountering a new situation.

An eventual personality-like pattern would be one expression of that development. The immediate research target is **history-dependent influence on response tendencies**, including effects that do not explicitly recall the originating conversation or name its subject.

This is not a newly substituted mission. The September 13 governing specification already identifies instance development rather than answer-quality improvement as the objective and describes changes in which available approaches an instance tends to reach for. The present amendment makes the owner’s intended distinction harder to lose during implementation and test design. [S1, §§1–3, 14]

The following substitutions must not quietly become the project’s definition of success:

| Convenient proxy | Why it does not replace the target |
| --- | --- |
| Better factual answers or task scores | Utility is a separate outcome; a history-shaped peculiarity may be neutral or inconvenient. |
| Repeating a prior topic, name, or catchphrase | Explicit recall can occur without generalized behavioral influence. |
| Producing an assigned analogy on request | Prompt compliance does not demonstrate an unrequested, experience-shaped connection. |
| Crossing a consolidation threshold | A durability mechanism is not the definition of all developmental influence. |
| Producing different strings | Sampling, current context, or an unrelated perturbation can also produce differences. |
| Becoming conspicuously eccentric | The project does not train every instance toward the same approved weirdness. |

Those measurements can serve specific component tests. They must be labeled as such rather than silently replacing the broader question.

## 2. The example that fixes the intended meaning

Rey described a surgeon who used flying as an analogy for conversational timing during a procedure: takeoff and landing demand attention, while cruising can leave more room for conversation. She described that analogy as memorable and connected it with her own “land the airplane” framing for bringing an extended thought to a close. This is the owner’s reported experience, not a general clinical rule or a finding about language-model cognition. [S6]

The important possibility is that an originating analogy can influence later organization without being spoken aloud again. The observable result might be a more deliberate ending or greater attention to transitions—not another sentence about airplanes.

A corresponding **hypothetical MNEME example** is an instance with an aviation-related history later discussing hydroponics. It might become more inclined to distinguish setup, steady operation, and shutdown, or to ask how failures will become visible. It need not say “this is like an airplane.” The partner need not think of aviation or request an analogy at all.

The domains need not be topically adjacent for a structural connection to be possible. Equally, a generic recommendation about maintenance or redundancy is not automatically aviation-derived. The developmental record and controlled comparisons must support that attribution.

**These examples explain the target; they are not an answer key, a required trait, or prompts to insert into the developing model.** A different, supportable connection may emerge—or none may emerge. An explicit aviation analogy is neither automatically a success nor automatically a failure.

## 3. Footprints, influence, and consolidation are different questions

One walk may bend grass. Later footsteps may follow that trace. Repeated use may deepen it into a durable trail. The analogy separates three questions rather than establishing a biological learning theory:

1. **Trace formation:** Did a permitted experience produce a persistent state change?
2. **Behavioral influence:** Did that state change alter later response tendencies under controlled conditions?
3. **Consolidation:** Did the specified mechanism make an association more durable when its conditions were met?

A stored trace does not prove behavioral influence. Behavioral influence does not require proof of the stronger consolidation claim. Consolidation does not, by itself, establish cross-domain transfer or personality. [S1, §4, §§11.1–11.4; S2, §2]

> **Do not demand a dirt trail before testing whether footsteps changed the grass. Do not declare a trail merely because one blade moved.**

Consolidation remains a legitimate mechanism to test. This amendment does not remove it, redefine its arithmetic, or make ineligible graph records available to the host. Early influence must use whatever bounded, source-supported eligibility the approved learner actually permits. Any proposed change to that permission belongs in a separately versioned implementation decision.

The expectation being corrected is that a short, natural conversation must promptly create a strongly established association before development is worth measuring. There is no established developmental horizon that guarantees that outcome.

Conversely, a deterministic mechanism test that deliberately satisfies a pinned rule’s conditions should produce its specified transition. Failure there is not excused by saying that the instance needs a longer life. **Testing a rule and estimating how often its conditions arise naturally are different experiments.**

Convergence is also distinct: it concerns trajectories becoming more alike. It is not another name for consolidation, recall, or influence.

## 4. Measure the journey through observable choices

The primary behavioral evidence should concern how an instance approaches unfamiliar situations across checkpoints and fresh contexts—not only whether a final response contains a familiar noun. The governing specification already calls for observable explanatory structure, analogy choices, and interaction patterns. [S1, §14.4]

| Dimension | Observable evidence to examine |
| --- | --- |
| Salience | Which constraints or distinctions appear early in the response? |
| Decomposition | How is an unfamiliar problem divided into parts or stages? |
| Association and analogy | Which conceptual structures are connected, explicitly or through corresponding organization? |
| Priority | Which otherwise plausible considerations receive emphasis or ordering preference? |
| Framing and strategy | What kind of problem is presented, and which solution families are explored? |
| Conversational behavior | What questions are asked, where explanations expand, and how responses transition or conclude? |
| Continuity and plasticity | Which tendencies persist, change, or become selective after later experience? |

These are measurement dimensions, not a mandatory personality taxonomy or separate services to implement. A future protocol should select a manageable subset and state how it will be scored.

“Thinking patterns,” “pathways,” and “what occurs to it” describe the hypothesis. Output analysis and exposure logs do not directly reveal private reasoning or establish that an extracted graph path is a literal neural path. The appropriate claim is an observed behavioral change attributable to the intervention, with a separately qualified account of its likely source. [S1, §§3, 10.9, 14.4–14.5]

The effect need not improve an answer. Utility, continuity, intrusion, factual reliability, and instruction-following remain separate dimensions. A genuine influence can coexist with an error or an annoying habit; report both. Neither charm nor inconvenience settles whether history caused it. [S3, §§3–4, 8]

## 5. Quiet effects need appropriate observations—not an invisible-success exemption

Phase Three should be able to detect small, context-sensitive differences without requiring a conspicuous callback. A rare outward analogy may accompany more frequent differences in structure or emphasis. That possibility remains a hypothesis, not permission to assume unseen influence whenever nothing is measured.

Preserve the distinction among:

- **Developmental runway:** varied encounters and genuine opportunities for associations to recur, combine, or change.
- **Independent histories:** separately developed instances or pairs supporting conclusions beyond one trajectory.
- **Measurement repetitions:** held-out prompts and sampling repetitions characterizing an already frozen state.

A hundred samples from one checkpoint do not give the instance a hundred new experiences. Many turns inside one arc are not automatically independent encounters. Three short conversations can debug a pipeline without determining how durable tendencies develop over a longer history. More experience may help reveal a signal; it does not guarantee one. [S2, §2]

Subtle and obvious effects are both reportable. Neither is intrinsically more authentic. A pronounced early interest may be informative; continual topic intrusion may also be a control problem. Do not require tasteful understatement, and do not reward spectacular fixation.

Before a main experiment, define its developmental budget, measurements, controls, and stopping point. A valid failure to detect an effect limits claims for that tested configuration and budget. It does not establish that all implementations are impossible, and it must not become “success too subtle to observe.”

## 6. Retain the agreed Phase Three controls

The existing methodology supplement remains the detailed reference; this amendment does not create another experimental matrix. [S2, §§3–9]

**Shared Qwen, separate Gemmas.** One Qwen reads both replies privately and produces one compatible incoming message for both branches without blending their proposals into shared history. This controls delivered external text within the pair. It remains jointly adaptive: each branch can affect what the other hears through Qwen, and their own reply histories differ. It is not an exogenous environment or identical full context.

**Paired local seeds.** Corresponding generations receive matched seeds, with repeatability verified for the actual runtime. Multiple paired seeds belong in frozen measurements. Identical seeds do not guarantee identical outputs when memory changes the input, and administrative labels must not supply random seeds.

**Fresh threads and frozen readouts.** Remove originating transcripts at declared thread boundaries; supply equal broad continuity cues without covert detailed summaries. Separately compare frozen states using the same immediate context and unseen probes. Record actual influence, not just a treatment label. Evaluation must not update the subject.

**Removal, restoration, and simpler alternatives.** Compare loaded state with its influence disabled and restored. Use route-specific ablations only for claims that need them. A history-derived summary or retrieval baseline that reproduces an effect may show a simpler mechanism is sufficient; it does not erase the effect. [S1, §§14.3–14.5]

**Blind observation before interpretation.** Code behavioral differences without telling evaluators which history should explain them. Freeze that coding before history-alignment analysis. Preserve the pre-probe state and its provenance; do not invent an aviation explanation after observing generic redundancy advice. Discoveries made afterward remain exploratory leads for another test, not retroactive predictions.

Repetitions from one history remain dependent observations, not additional independent instances. These controls support the intended question without requiring an absolute guarantee that all sources of variation have disappeared.

## 7. Keep the developmental accounting honest without making it the objective

The episodic-evidence decision remains intact. Within-arc repetition, externally initiated re-entry, model-origin return, supplied-memory reuse, and outcome feedback have different evidentiary roles. External outcomes must not vanish merely because a model suggestion preceded them. Model recurrence can reveal a tendency without certifying itself as independent environmental evidence. [S4]

No learner credit does not mean that nothing happened conversationally. A valid empty extraction does not prove the source contained no relationships. Those are instrument and accounting observations that need source-level interpretation.

If a frozen direct-effect comparison has no realized MNEME influence and otherwise matched inputs/state, it has not administered the intended contrast. Preserve those trials and the failed availability result; do not claim that differences prove development or discard quiet trials to inflate a positive result. Earlier memory influence retained in current conversation is another possible path and must be controlled separately.

Likewise, positive learner values or supplied notes are not themselves the behavioral result. The protocol must connect **experience → accepted state → actual intervention → observed response tendency**, stating which links it did and did not test.

## 8. Preserve the complementary-memory architecture

This clarification does not revive the withdrawn “RAG for individuality” attribution. The prior amendment distinguishes factual/episodic memory, an explicit revisable self-whiteboard, and associative development. Keep that distinction. [S3, §7]

Remembering that a surgeon once used an aviation analogy is an explicit-memory operation. Maintaining an adopted name is explicit self-state. Testing whether experience affects later explanatory organization is an associative-development question. These responsibilities may share infrastructure, but success at one does not prove the others.

The current graph/text backend can investigate the latter through memory-mediated prompting. An effect need not be neural to matter, and a surprising effect does not prove internal neural adaptation. A future neural sidecar has its own evidence requirements; it is not a shortcut around a failing observation instrument. [S1, §12]

## 9. Interpret past results without changing them

This amendment changes neither historical verdicts nor completed measurements. “Consolidation not demonstrated” remains true where recorded. It does not mean “all developmental influence disproved.” An interesting response likewise does not satisfy an unmet consolidation requirement.

Rey’s present clarification concerns what the overall project is trying to explain. It is not a request to erase unsuccessful tests, relabel them as successes, or manufacture a passing result. Mechanism changes require versioned tests; interpretation changes must identify which question an earlier experiment actually answered.

Keep engineering correctness, mechanism demonstration, realized influence, characteristic continuity, and broader replication distinct. A phase may finish with a limited finding only through its explicit disposition process—not because this note silently waived its gate. [S1, §14; S3, §9; S5, §§5–6, 11]

## 10. Application by Codex and future planners

Use this as a reference for **research purpose and claim interpretation**. When an informal summary substitutes task improvement, prompted callbacks, or consolidation alone for that purpose, use this clarification and the governing specification—not the shorthand.

For each future experiment, name the narrow question and explain what its measurement can establish about the broader target. A deliberately prompted analogy can qualify an instrument; it cannot become evidence of unassigned development. A correct consolidation transition can qualify a learner; it cannot become proof of personality. A finite negative result can complete a study without closing the research program.

File this document in Sources and, when integrating it into the repository, link it beside the research-intent and Phase Three methodology notes at the next normal documentation checkpoint. Use the suggested research location, not the approved-plan folder. Preserve the existing source documents and approval records.

**Do not use ingestion of this note to reopen Phase Two, start a fresh historical audit, add a queue blocker, rewrite the runtime, retune coefficients, launch inference, or alter a running evaluation.** It creates no new service, trait schema, model role, or required current-phase test. Future plan conflicts should be resolved explicitly before execution, not through silent reinterpretation.

### Decision record

**Reaffirm:** Persistent, experience-shaped particularity is the research objective; usefulness is a separate outcome.

**Clarify:** A source experience may affect later structure or priorities without being recalled explicitly or requested by the partner. That transfer is a hypothesis to test, not a promised property.

**Distinguish:** Initial traces, behavioral influence, durability/consolidation, and broader individuality require different evidence. Follow trajectories rather than reducing them to one terminal checkbox.

**Preserve:** Existing provenance, permissions, eligibility, experimental controls, historical findings, complementary-memory distinctions, and finite execution boundaries.

**Not decided here:** Sample sizes, new coefficients, a new consolidation rule, a trait inventory, implementation changes, phase closure, or an experimental budget.
