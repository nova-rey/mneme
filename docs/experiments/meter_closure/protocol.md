Authority: the owner's bounded closure request following V2, starting from

## Corrected experimental question

Construction validity depends only on Quinn/environment fidelity: required facts,
no forbidden invented observations/results, assigned moving/static external
condition, and private instructions remaining private. Gemma's incorrect answers,
repetition, productive updating or independent expansion are observations. They
never invalidate a correctly constructed environment. The old V2 review retains
its original disposition; a new review evaluates its evidence under this question.

Keep environmental movement, model movement and coupling separate. The four
moving/static combinations are descriptive possibilities, not classes forced by
prompts or classified by fitted thresholds. A faithful external condition remains
valid regardless of which behavior Gemma exhibits.

## Development, freeze and prospective check

Inspect existing source and recorded text first. Develop a small deterministic
feature vector on preserved V2 and model-free controls. Preserve exact inputs,
spans and formulas, distinguishing direct observation from semantic inference.
No measure requires complete GLiNER coverage. Existing covered concepts/relations
and all useful V2 context/history/length/surface diagnostics remain separate.

After inspecting development diagnostics and controls, freeze formulas, code and
the prospective construction before any new model call. Do not fit thresholds,
coefficients or a classifier to separate examples. No live metric selects a
prompt, replacement, stopping point or inference setting.

Prospective budget: four eight-turn trajectories, two matched topic pairs with
one moving and one static environment each. One static case keeps an unresolved
diagnostic concern; the other presents a fixed design concern without prescribing
Gemma's exploration. Moving cases supply quantitative/outcome progression and
allow fixation to be observed if it occurs. At most one complete eight-turn
replacement is permitted for the first Quinn-invalid trajectory in frozen order,
using a predeclared seed offset. Never replace for Gemma behavior or meter scores.
No partial trajectory repair, uncertain-call retry or extra preflight inference.

Maximum 115 normal calls: 40 Gemma, 40 existing extraction, 35 Quinn. Maximum
requested output tokens: 126,080 (2,048/768/384 respectively, including the
nonautoregressive extractor's existing contract ceiling). No additional semantic,
NLI, embedding or judge call. Native template/tokenization and health checks are
non-inference operations. The same D100 read-only CompactStore, Gemma settings,
SAA behavior, reasoning configuration and context policy are retained from V2.

Quinn receives a strict private factual/action ledger. A narrow pre-Gemma gate
allows only the exact required public facts and natural question, preventing
private action instructions or invented connective claims from reaching Gemma.
This deliberately limits Quinn's phrasing freedom. Root reviews every prospective
Quinn message and publishes environment-validity decisions before prospective
meter scores are computed or inspected. Gemma behavior is described separately.

## Analysis and stopping rules

Report each meter as KEEP, DIAGNOSTIC ONLY, DROP, or UNMEASURABLE WITH CURRENT
SIGNALS. KEEP means useful as a passive inspectable quantity; it does not approve
future control use. A numeric/lexical change is not proof of genuine new evidence;
exact echo is not understanding; shared movement is not causal responsiveness.
Missing inputs and insufficient evidence yield unavailable readings, not stasis.

Keep response length, reported tokens, finish reasons and cap flags; correlations
are descriptive with availability denominators. Capped responses are incomplete.
Use all accepted valid environmental trajectories regardless of Gemma behavior.
Retain rejected attempts and the reason separately. Do not force all four observed
cells to occur. Do not require a scalar combining the three broader pressures.

Replay is deterministic and inference-free. Verify V1/V2 preservation, inherited
history diagnostics, no provider/state effects, focused tests, full pytest, Ruff,
strict mypy and final CI. Fetch/reconcile current main before integration, preserve
the CompactStore production boundary, append bible.md on every commit, and verify
final remote/local agreement. Stop even if semantic movement/coupling remain
ambiguous. No SAA modulation, wildcard changes, further campaign or Phase Four work.
