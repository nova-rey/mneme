# MNEME Phase Two Operational Budget Contingency Addendum

**Status:** Active operational amendment  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-24  
**Scope:** Phase Two execution accounting only

This addendum does not change the approved Phase Two experiment, scientific
acceptance criteria, learner rules, model roles, cohort, fixtures, or release
requirements. It treats the approved provider-call ceiling as runaway
protection while preserving cumulative, attributable accounting.

For already-authorized, in-scope completion, qualification, repair, or ordinary
execution variance, Codex may continue without a new authorization when there
is no new scientific condition, cohort, model role, or objective; no
retry-until-success or favorable resampling occurs; every call is fixed before
dispatch and durably recorded; cost remains trivial; and interpretation is not
materially changed.

The contingency increment is the larger of **100 calls** or **25% of the
approved campaign ceiling**. For the approved Phase Two ceiling of 299 calls,
25% is 74.75, so the operational cumulative cap is **399 calls**. This is a
maximum envelope, not a target. Historical calls remain counted exactly once,
and unused allowance must not be consumed merely because it exists.

The approved maximum output-token ceiling remains **204,288**. This amendment
does not increase it. A continuation must reserve and enforce that ceiling
independently of the call contingency.

Stop for review if projected work exceeds the contingency envelope, approaches
approximately 1,000 calls without explicit scale approval, makes cost
non-trivial, adds a model/provider role, condition, cohort, or scientific
question, indicates architectural redesign, or becomes stochastic resampling.

For the current P2.3 campaign, 42 historical calls are reconciled. The 34-call
shortfall against the nominal no-repair schedule is inside this authority; the
remaining unresolved extraction-stop review still requires an in-scope
engineering/recovery decision and is not waived by this accounting amendment.
